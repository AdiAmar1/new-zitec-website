About Us

Leaders in Israel in electronic enclosures and prototype development since 2000

Zitec – Enclosure & Technologies Ltd. was founded in 2000 and is a leader in Israel in electronic enclosures, prototype development, model manufacturing, and production series for defense, medical, and civilian industries. The company operates with professionalism and high engineering innovation, with uncompromising quality, exceptionally short timelines, and comprehensive solutions tailored to each customer.

Zitec is certified to the international quality standard ISO 9001:2015 and employs a skilled team of about 45 employees in engineering, production, quality control, and management. Over the years, the company has established itself as a reliable, valued supplier to leading organizations, including the Ministry of Defense, the Prime Minister's Office, the Atomic Energy Commission, Elbit divisions, Orbotech, Novamil, HP, Aeronautics, Astronautics, Landa, Rotem, Degesh, and many others.

The company uses advanced metalworking technologies and holds innovative equipment including high-precision fiber laser cutting machines (up to 25 mm thick), bending and punching machines, welding of all material types, polishing, hardware insertion, a die-making department, milling, and 3-axis CNC machining. In addition, Zitec provides full engineering support during design and development, guidance in prototype production, and solutions tailored to special requirements.

The factory uses a wide range of high-quality raw materials such as titanium, copper, brass, aluminum, stainless steel, steel, phosphor bronze, glass epoxy, polycarbonate, and Mylar. Alongside manufacturing services, the company also performs engraving, laser marking, coating, painting, and printing — all with a high finish quality and strict supervision.

Since its founding, Zitec has specialized in combining engineering precision, technological innovation, and personal service, while upholding reliability, professionalism, and excellence. Thanks to the knowledge, equipment, and experience accumulated over the years, the company continues to be a strategic partner for leading Israeli companies and a leader in enclosure and metalworking in Israel.

Our Clients

We serve leading companies across the market. Our clients include: the Ministry of Defense, the Prime Minister's Office, D.Z. 2470, the Atomic Energy Commission, all Elbit divisions (Elta, Aircraft, Elop, and more), Orbotech, Matan, Novatech, Novamil, Shimcoteq, HP, Aeronautics, Astronautics, Landa, Rotem, Degesh, and many others.

Zitec – Enclosure & Technologies Ltd. specializes in manufacturing electronic enclosures, prototypes, models, and production series for the medical industry, the printing industry, and more, as well as die making and press work.
