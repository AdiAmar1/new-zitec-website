Zitec - Enclosure & Technologies Ltd.

Zitec – Enclosure & Technologies Ltd. specializes in electronic enclosures,
prototypes, models, and production series for the medical, printing, and other industries, as well as die making and press work
with a strong focus on high quality and exceptionally short lead times.

What We Offer

01. Manufacturing with World-Class Technologies

Including fiber laser cutting machines that significantly shorten production times and enable high cutting precision up to 25 mm. Bending machines, punching machines, welding of all material types, polishing machines, hardware insertion machines, a die-making department, a milling department, and a 3-axis CNC machining department. Technical support and solutions during design stages and prototype manufacturing.

02. Manufacturing Materials

The company uses a wide range of materials, including titanium, copper, brass, aluminum, stainless steel, steel, bronze, Mylar, polycarbonate, glass epoxy, and more.

03.Additional Services

Engraving / scoring and laser marking are performed on-site. Coating, painting, and printing processes are carried out via subcontractors. Maintenance — company machines are serviced and maintained to the highest standard.
